Many people now search Instagram the way they search Google. They type "bridal lehenga Ahmedabad" or "home bakery Surat" and browse the results. Instagram SEO means setting up your profile and posts so they appear in those searches.

The good news is that most of it is free and quick. The work is in using the right words in the right places.

## Where Instagram reads your words

Instagram matches searches against several parts of your account:

1. **Username** (@shreetextiles)
2. **Name field**, the bold name on your profile
3. **Bio**
4. **Captions**, especially the first lines
5. **Hashtags**
6. **Text on Reels and spoken words**, which Instagram can read and transcribe
7. **Alt text** on images
8. **Location tags**

## Put a keyword in your name field

The name field is searchable and separate from your username. Most businesses waste it by repeating their brand. Add what you do and where:

- `Shree Textiles | Silk Sarees Surat`
- `Dr. Mehta | Dentist in Vadodara`
- `Crumbs & Co | Home Bakery Ahmedabad`

You have a limited number of characters, and Instagram restricts how often you can change the name, so choose carefully. The [Rankdesk hashtag generator](/hashtag-generator) builds a name field and bio for you and shows the character count.

## Write a bio for people and search

Your bio should answer three questions in a few short lines: what you offer, where, and what to do next.

```
Factory-direct pure silk sarees
📍 Surat, India · Ships across India
👉 DM for the wholesale price list
```

Read our [Instagram bio guide](/blog/instagram-bio-guide) for more examples by type of business.

## Captions: lead with the keyword

Write captions the way people search. Instead of "New arrivals! 😍", try "New Banarasi silk sarees for the wedding season, handwoven in Varanasi". The first line also shows in the feed before "more", so it has to work for people too.

Keep it natural. A caption written only for search reads like spam, and people scroll past.

## Hashtags still help, differently

Hashtags now act more like labels that tell Instagram what a post is about than a way to go viral. Instagram has been cutting back on how many hashtags it allows per post, so check the current limit in the app. A small, relevant set beats a block of thirty. Mix:

- **Niche tags** for what the post shows: #banarasisaree
- **Local tags** for where you are: #suratfashion
- **A branded tag** for your business: #shreetextiles

Our [hashtag guide](/blog/instagram-hashtags-guide) covers how to choose them.

## Reels: say it and show it

Instagram can read text on screen and transcribe speech in Reels. If your Reel is about "how to drape a silk saree", say those words and put them on screen in the first seconds. Add captions, which also help the many people watching without sound.

## Alt text and location

- Go to **Advanced settings** before posting and write alt text that describes the image: "Red Banarasi silk saree with gold zari border". It helps accessibility and search.
- Tag your city or area on posts. Local searchers browse location pages.

## Getting found in Google too

Instagram allows public posts from professional accounts to be shown in search engines such as Google. If you want this, check your account's privacy settings and keep the account public. Keyword-rich captions then help in both places.

## Signals that matter beyond keywords

Keywords get you into the results. What keeps you there is how people react: saves, shares, comments, watch time on Reels and follows from your profile. Post regularly, reply to comments and DMs, and make content people want to keep. A searchable profile with nothing worth following won't grow.

## A quick checklist

- Keyword and city in your name field
- Clear bio with offer, location and call to action
- Keyword in the first line of each caption
- A few relevant hashtags, rotated
- Alt text and a location tag on every post
- Spoken and on-screen keywords in Reels
