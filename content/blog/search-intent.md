Two searches can share the same words and want completely different things. "Silk saree" from someone ready to buy needs a product page with prices. "Silk saree care" needs a guide. Search intent is the reason behind a search, and matching it is one of the biggest factors in whether a page can rank at all.

If Google shows ten guides for a keyword, a product page won't break in, however well optimised it is. Google has learned what people want from that search, and it shows them that.

## The four types of intent

| Intent | The searcher wants to | Typical words | Best page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informational | learn something | how, what, why, guide, ideas | Guide or blog post |
| Commercial | compare options before buying | best, top, vs, review, alternatives | Comparison, review or list |
| Transactional | buy, book or sign up | buy, price, order, book, hire, quote | Product, service or pricing page |
| Navigational | reach a specific site | brand names, "login" | Your home or login page |

Local searches ("near me", "in Ahmedabad") are usually transactional or commercial with a place attached. They need a page, and a Google Business Profile, that clearly serve that area.

## How to check intent for any keyword

Don't guess. Search the keyword in Google, ideally in a private window, and look at the first page:

1. **What type of pages rank?** Product pages, category pages, guides, videos, local map results?
2. **What format?** Lists ("10 best…"), step-by-step guides, comparisons, calculators?
3. **What angle?** Cheap, premium, for beginners, current year?
4. **What else does Google show?** A map pack means local intent. Video results mean people want to watch. "People also ask" boxes show follow-up questions you should answer.

Whatever most of page one has in common is the intent you need to match.

## Examples

- **"how to identify pure silk saree"**: informational. The results are guides and videos. Write a clear guide with the tests and photos, and link to your products at the end.
- **"best saree shop in surat"**: commercial and local. The results show the map pack and lists. Strengthen your Business Profile and reviews, and consider a page explaining what makes your shop different.
- **"banarasi silk saree price"**: transactional. The results are product and category pages with prices. Show prices or price ranges clearly.
- **"waterproofing vs tiling terrace"**: commercial. People are comparing. A fair comparison page with costs and pros and cons fits.

## Mixed intent

Some searches have mixed results, say half guides and half product pages. That means people want different things. You can target it with the type that suits your business, or build a page that serves both, such as a category page with a short buying guide at the top.

## Matching intent on the page

Once you know the intent, make it obvious from the first screen:

- **The title and H1** should promise what the searcher wants: "Banarasi Silk Sarees, Prices from ₹4,999" rather than "Our Collection".
- **The first paragraph** should deliver it quickly. No long introductions before the answer.
- **The format** should match: steps for how-to searches, a table for comparisons, prices and a buy button for transactional ones.
- **Next steps** should follow naturally: a guide links to products, a product page links to care guides.

## Intent explains traffic problems

If a page gets impressions in Search Console but few clicks, or clicks but people leave fast, check intent. A common cause is a page answering a different question from the one people searched. Rewriting the page to match is often more effective than any technical fix.
