Local SEO is how a business shows up when people nearby search for what it offers, especially in the map results at the top of Google. For shops, clinics, restaurants, contractors and most service businesses, it matters more than any other kind of SEO.

Google says local rankings depend mainly on three things: **relevance** (how well your listing matches the search), **distance** (how close you are to the searcher or the place they named) and **prominence** (how well known and well reviewed you are). You can't move your shop, but you can improve the other two. Work through this list.

## Google Business Profile

- [ ] Profile claimed and verified. Follow our [setup guide](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) if not.
- [ ] Business name exactly as on your signboard, no added keywords
- [ ] Most specific primary category, plus relevant secondary categories
- [ ] Map pin on your entrance
- [ ] Phone number answered during business hours
- [ ] Opening hours correct, including festival and holiday hours
- [ ] Description that says what you do and where, written for customers
- [ ] Every main product or service listed
- [ ] At least 10 real photos: shopfront, inside, team, products or work
- [ ] New photos and a post added every month
- [ ] Questions on the profile answered

## Reviews

- [ ] A short review link saved and ready to send on WhatsApp
- [ ] Every happy customer asked, at the moment they're happiest
- [ ] Every review replied to, positive and negative
- [ ] No paid, fake or incentivised reviews (see [how to get reviews within the rules](/blog/google-reviews-guide))

## Your website

- [ ] Name, address and phone in the footer of every page, as text, not an image
- [ ] A contact page with an embedded Google map and directions from landmarks
- [ ] Home page [title tag](/blog/title-tag-guide) with your main service and city
- [ ] A separate page for each main service
- [ ] If you serve several cities or areas, a genuinely useful page for each, with local details, not copy-pasted text with the city name swapped
- [ ] [LocalBusiness schema](/blog/local-business-schema) on the home or contact page
- [ ] Click-to-call phone number and a WhatsApp button on mobile
- [ ] Fast on mobile data. Many local searches happen on the go.

## Citations and consistency

A citation is any mention of your business name, address and phone online.

- [ ] Same name, address and phone everywhere: your website, Google, JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, Facebook, Instagram and industry directories
- [ ] Old addresses and numbers updated after a move
- [ ] Duplicate listings removed or merged

Inconsistent details confuse search engines and customers. Our [NAP consistency guide](/blog/nap-consistency) shows how to audit them.

## Local reputation

- [ ] Listed in your trade association and local chamber of commerce directories
- [ ] Suppliers and partners link to your website
- [ ] Mentions in local news, events or sponsorships
- [ ] Local content on your website, such as projects in specific areas or guides for local customers

## Language and search habits

- [ ] Think about how local customers search. Many type in Hinglish ("saree shop near me surat") or their own language. See our guide to [Hindi and Gujarati keywords](/blog/hindi-gujarati-keywords).
- [ ] Include landmarks and area names customers use, such as "near Iscon Cross Road" or "Ring Road market".

## Mistakes that hold Indian businesses back

1. **Several listings for one shop**, often created by staff or customers over the years.
2. **Keyword-stuffed names** like "Best Dentist Vadodara Dental Clinic Painless". These risk suspension.
3. **A mobile number nobody answers.** Missed calls become reviews like "never picks up".
4. **No website, or a website with no address.** The profile and website support each other.
5. **Only asking family for reviews.** Google filters reviews that look unnatural.

## Measure it

In your Business Profile, check calls, direction requests and website clicks each month. In Search Console, look at searches that include your city or "near me". Rising numbers mean the checklist is working.
