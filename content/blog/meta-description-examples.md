The meta description is the grey text under your blue title in Google results. It doesn't directly change where you rank, but it strongly affects whether people click. Think of it as a short advert for the page.

## How long should it be?

Google trims descriptions that are too wide, usually somewhere around 150 to 160 characters on desktop and less on mobile. Put the most important information in the first 120 characters so it survives either way.

## Will Google use mine?

Not always. Google often shows a different sentence from the page when it thinks that sentence matches the search better. You can't force it, but a clear, relevant description that matches the page's main topic is used far more often.

## A simple formula

**What the page offers + a detail that matters to the searcher + a reason to act**

The details that matter are usually price, location, speed, proof or something free.

## 12 examples

**Shops and products**

1. *Silk sarees:* "Pure Banarasi and Kanjivaram silk sarees direct from our Surat looms. Wholesale rates for dealers and delivery across India."
2. *Kitchen sinks:* "Stainless steel kitchen sinks in 30+ sizes, with 10-year rust warranty. Factory prices, free delivery in Gujarat."
3. *Home bakery:* "Eggless custom cakes in Ahmedabad, baked to order. Order on WhatsApp by 2 pm for same-day delivery."

**Services**

4. *Waterproofing:* "Terrace and bathroom waterproofing in Ahmedabad with a 5-year written warranty. Free site inspection within 48 hours."
5. *Dentist:* "Painless root canals, braces and implants in Vadodara. Open Sundays. Book online or call for an appointment today."
6. *CA firm:* "GST registration, returns and income tax filing for small businesses in Rajkot. Fixed monthly fees, no surprises."

**Tools and software**

7. *IP lookup:* "Find the city, country and internet provider of any IP address. Free, instant and no signup needed."
8. *Invoice app:* "Create GST invoices on your phone in under a minute. Free for up to 50 invoices a month."
9. *SEO audit:* "Check any page for title, meta tags, schema, speed and crawl problems. Free SEO audit with a fix list in 20 seconds."

**Blog posts and guides**

10. *How-to:* "Learn the burn test, the ring test and four other ways to check whether a saree is pure silk before you buy."
11. *Comparison:* "Waterproofing coating or tiling: we compare cost, lifespan and leak risk for Indian terraces, with real prices."
12. *Checklist:* "A 20-point local SEO checklist for shops and service businesses, from Google Business Profile to reviews."

Notice that none of them repeat a keyword awkwardly, and every one gives the reader something specific.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving it empty.** Google then picks any text from the page, often your menu or cookie notice.
- **Copying one description across many pages.** Every page should have its own.
- **Keyword lists.** "Sarees, silk sarees, buy sarees, sarees Surat" looks spammy and gets ignored.
- **Promising what the page doesn't deliver.** People bounce back to the results, and Google notices pages that disappoint.
- **Quotes in the code.** A double quote `"` inside the tag can cut it off. Use single quotes or rewrite.

## Where to set it

In WordPress use the meta description field in Yoast, Rank Math or a similar plugin. Shopify and Wix have a search preview section on each page and product. In raw HTML it goes in the `<head>`:

```html
<meta name="description" content="Pure Banarasi silk sarees direct from Surat looms...">
```
