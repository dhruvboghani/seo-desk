If you sell products online, Product schema is the structured data with the most visible payoff. It can let Google show your price, whether the item is in stock and its rating directly in search results and in Google's shopping features. Those details help shoppers choose your result before they click.

## What Product schema can show

Depending on the page and the search, Google may show:

- price and currency
- availability, such as "In stock"
- star rating and review count
- shipping and return information
- the product in Google's shopping surfaces

Google decides when to show these. Valid markup makes a page eligible, but doesn't guarantee it.

## A complete example

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Product",
  "name": "Red Banarasi Silk Saree with Gold Zari",
  "image": [
    "https://shreetextiles.in/images/red-banarasi-front.jpg",
    "https://shreetextiles.in/images/red-banarasi-pallu.jpg"
  ],
  "description": "Handwoven pure silk Banarasi saree with gold zari border and matching blouse piece.",
  "sku": "BNR-RED-014",
  "brand": { "@type": "Brand", "name": "Shree Textiles" },
  "offers": {
    "@type": "Offer",
    "url": "https://shreetextiles.in/sarees/red-banarasi-silk",
    "price": "8499",
    "priceCurrency": "INR",
    "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock",
    "itemCondition": "https://schema.org/NewCondition"
  },
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.7",
    "reviewCount": "38"
  }
}
```

## The fields that matter most

- **name:** the product name as shown on the page.
- **image:** one or more high-quality product photos. Google prefers several aspect ratios when available.
- **offers:** the price block, with:
  - **price:** a number only, such as `8499`, with no currency symbol or commas.
  - **priceCurrency:** `INR` for rupees.
  - **availability:** `InStock`, `OutOfStock`, `PreOrder` or `BackOrder`, as a schema.org URL.
- **description, sku, brand:** help identify the product exactly.
- **gtin or mpn:** if your products have barcodes (GTIN/EAN) or manufacturer part numbers, include them.
- **aggregateRating and review:** only when real customer reviews are shown on the page.

## Rules that keep it trustworthy

1. **Match the visible page.** The price and stock in the markup must be what the shopper sees. If the page says ₹7,999 on sale, the markup can't say ₹8,499.
2. **Update it automatically.** Prices and stock change. Markup generated by your store platform from your product data stays accurate. Hand-written markup goes stale.
3. **Real reviews only.** Ratings must come from customers, be visible on the page, and not be selectively chosen.
4. **One product per product page.** Category pages listing many products shouldn't mark each up as a full Product in the main position.

## Platforms

- **Shopify:** most themes output Product schema. Check it with the Rich Results Test, and avoid adding a second app that duplicates it.
- **WooCommerce:** outputs Product schema, and SEO plugins can extend it.
- **Custom stores:** generate the JSON-LD from the same data that renders the page, so they never disagree.

## Merchant listings

Beyond schema, you can upload a product feed to Google Merchant Center, which can show your products in Google Shopping for free. Schema and the feed work together, and Merchant Center reports mismatches between them.

## Test it

Use Google's Rich Results Test on a few product pages, and watch the Product snippets and Merchant listings reports in Search Console for errors such as "Missing field price" or "Invalid availability value".
