When someone lands on your Instagram profile, they decide in a few seconds whether to follow, message you or leave. Your name field and bio do most of the work in those seconds. They also help people find you in Instagram search in the first place.

## The two fields that matter

- **Name field:** the bold name above your bio. It's searchable, so it should include what you do, not just your brand.
- **Bio:** the short text under it, limited to 150 characters. It tells visitors what you offer and what to do next.

Your **username** (@handle) is searchable too, but changing it can confuse existing followers and break old links, so keep it stable once set.

## The name field formula

**Brand | What you do + City**

- `Shree Textiles | Silk Sarees Surat`
- `Bhanderi Buildcon | Builders Ahmedabad`
- `Dr. Kavya Mehta | Dentist Vadodara`
- `Crumbs & Co | Eggless Cakes Rajkot`

If you sell online across India, swap the city for your main product detail: `Loom Story | Handloom Cotton Sarees`. Instagram limits how often you can change the name field, so decide carefully.

## The bio formula

Three short lines usually work best:

1. **What you offer**, in customer words, with a key detail
2. **Proof or location**, such as city, years, delivery area or a credential
3. **A call to action** pointing to your link, DMs or WhatsApp

## Examples by business type

**Manufacturer or wholesaler**
```
Factory-direct pure silk sarees since 1998
📍 Surat · Ships across India
👉 DM "PRICE" for the wholesale list
```

**Service business**
```
House construction & renovation, on time
🏗 120+ homes built in Ahmedabad
📞 Free site visit, tap Call
```

**Clinic**
```
Painless dental care for the whole family
📍 Alkapuri, Vadodara · Open Sundays
🗓 Book your appointment below
```

**Home bakery**
```
Eggless custom cakes & dessert boxes
📍 Rajkot · Same-day delivery before 2 pm
👇 Order on WhatsApp
```

**Online tool or app**
```
Find any IP address location, free
⚡ City, ISP and map in one click
🔗 Try it at the link below
```

Emojis can make a bio easier to scan, but use them as signposts, not decoration. One per line is plenty.

## Make the most of your link

You get a limited number of links in your bio. Point the main link to the page that matches your call to action, such as a WhatsApp chat, your product catalogue or a booking page, rather than always the home page. If you use a link-in-bio page with several links, keep it short and put the most important link first.

## Use the other profile features

- **Category:** choose the business category that fits best. It shows under your name.
- **Contact buttons:** add call, email, WhatsApp and directions for a business account.
- **Story highlights:** create highlights for products, prices, reviews, FAQs and how to order. They act like menu tabs on your profile.
- **Pinned posts:** pin your best introduction, a customer review and your current offer.

## Common mistakes

- A name field with only the brand name, missing the chance to be found
- A bio full of hashtags, which eats the few characters you have and sends visitors away to other people's posts
- Vague lines such as "Living the dream ✨" for a business account
- No call to action, so interested visitors don't know what to do
- A broken or outdated link
