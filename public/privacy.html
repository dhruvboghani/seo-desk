Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information {{SITE_NAME}} ({{SITE_URL}}) collects when you use the site, and how advertising on the site works.

Information you enter

When you audit a URL, our server fetches that public page and sends the URL to Google's PageSpeed Insights service to measure speed. We do not save the URLs you audit or the results. Text you type into the generators (meta tags, schema, hashtags, keywords, sitemaps) is processed in your browser and is not sent to our server.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our server records your IP address briefly to prevent abuse by limiting how many audits can run per minute. Your browser stores the last tool tab you opened, so the site reopens on it. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
