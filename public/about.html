About {{SITE_NAME}}

A free SEO toolkit for marketers, agencies and small business owners.

SEO work usually means jumping between a dozen websites: one for meta tags, one for page speed, another for schema, another for hashtags. {{SITE_NAME}} puts the everyday checks and generators in one place, so you can audit a page and fix what it finds without leaving the tab.

What you can do

  • Audit any URL. Check the title, meta description, headings, image alt text, canonical tag, Open Graph tags and schema, plus the HTTP status, redirects, HTTPS, robots.txt, XML sitemap and Google PageSpeed scores.
  • Write meta tags with a live preview of how the page will look in Google results.
  • Generate schema markup (JSON-LD) for local businesses, organizations, products, FAQs and articles.
  • Build a social pack: Instagram hashtags, profile name and bio, and YouTube tags.
  • Find keyword ideas in local, commercial, buying and question forms.
  • Create robots.txt and sitemap.xml files ready to upload.

How the audit works

When you enter a URL, our server downloads that page's HTML the same way a search engine crawler would, then checks it against widely used on-page SEO guidelines. Speed and Core Web Vitals come from Google's PageSpeed Insights service. The score is a quick health check: it shows which technical problems may hold a page back, but rankings also depend on content quality, links from other sites and how well the page answers the search.
