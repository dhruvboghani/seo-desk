Upload, then test
Open yourdomain.com/robots.txt in a browser to check it loads. Then audit a few pages here to confirm they are not blocked by mistake.

      
Submit the sitemap
In Google Search Console, open Sitemaps, enter sitemap.xml and submit. Search Console then reports how many pages Google has indexed.

      
Never block CSS or JS
Google needs your stylesheets and scripts to see the page the way visitors do. Block only private areas such as admin, cart and checkout.
