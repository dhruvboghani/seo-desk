Writing a title that gets clicks
Put the main search phrase first, then what makes you different, then the brand. "Pure Silk Sarees from Surat | Shree Textiles" beats "Home | Shree Textiles".

      
A description that sells
Answer the search in one sentence, then give a reason to click: price, delivery, free trial or location. Keep it under the green line in the width meter.
