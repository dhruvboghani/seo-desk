Traffic figures are Similarweb estimates. Compare websites and trends rather than reading them as exact counts.

Compare, don't count

Estimates are best for comparing your site with competitors and spotting trends over months. For exact numbers on your own site, use Google Analytics and Search Console.

Steal the keyword gaps

Open a competitor's top keywords and look for searches you have no page for. Then use keyword ideas to plan the page.

Watch AI traffic

If competitors get visits from ChatGPT or Perplexity and you don't, check which of their pages AI assistants cite, and make yours clearer and more complete.
