Rank, not visits

Popularity rank compares websites with each other. A rank of 50,000 means about 49,999 websites are more popular. Use it to compare competitors, not to estimate visits.

Your own numbers are exact

Search Console shows the searches that bring people to your site. Analytics shows every visit, from every source. Together they answer what no outside tool can.

Watch AI visitors

If ChatGPT or Perplexity send you visitors, look at which pages they land on. Make those pages clearer and more complete, and write more like them.
