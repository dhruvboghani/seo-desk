One page per intent
"Silk saree price" and "how to identify pure silk" need different pages. Buying searches want a product page. Questions want a guide.

      
Start long and local
New sites rarely rank for short, broad words. A product plus a city, or a specific question, is where you can win first.

      
Use the words in the right places
Put the main phrase in the title tag, the H1 and the first paragraph, and use related phrases in your H2 headings.
