One desk for everyday SEO.

Rankdesk is a free toolkit for marketers, agencies and small business owners who are tired of jumping between a dozen websites.

Why we built it

Everyday SEO work means checking a page in one tool, writing meta tags in another, building schema in a third and looking up hashtags somewhere else. Rankdesk puts those checks and generators in one place. You audit a page, see what to fix first, and open a tool that is already filled in with your site's details.

What you can do

  • Audit any URL for titles, meta tags, headings, image alt text, canonical tags, schema, HTTP status, redirects, HTTPS, robots.txt, the XML sitemap and Google PageSpeed.
  • Write meta tags with a live preview of the search result and the WhatsApp or LinkedIn share card.
  • Generate schema markup for local businesses, organizations, products, FAQs and articles.
  • Build a social pack of hashtags, an Instagram name and bio, and YouTube tags.
  • Find keyword ideas in local, comparison, buying and question forms.
  • Create robots.txt and sitemap.xml files ready to upload.
  • Learn the basics from our guides, written for people who run websites rather than for SEO specialists.

How the audit works

When you enter a URL, our server downloads the page's HTML the way a search engine crawler does and checks it against widely accepted on-page guidelines. Speed and Core Web Vitals come from Google's PageSpeed Insights service. The score is a health check. It shows technical problems that can hold a page back, but rankings also depend on how well the content answers the search, links from other websites and, for local businesses, the Google Business Profile.

Who builds it

Rankdesk is built and maintained by a small team of web developers in Ahmedabad, India. Many of the examples come from the businesses we work with every day: textile makers, contractors, clinics, shops and online tools.

How it is funded

Every tool is free, with no signup and no limit. The site is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Ads are always labelled and never placed inside tool results.
