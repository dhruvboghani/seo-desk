Mix your hashtags
Niche tags reach people interested in what you sell. Local tags reach people nearby. Your branded tag collects customer posts in one place. Use some of each.

      
Write for search
Instagram reads the first line of a caption and the words spoken or shown in Reels. Use the same keywords there, written naturally.

      
Rotate sets
Posting the same hashtags on every post can look like spam. Keep three or four sets and rotate them depending on the post.
